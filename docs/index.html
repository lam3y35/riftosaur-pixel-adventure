What's New in v1.3.0

60 FPS & Expanded Touch Zones

Locked 60 FPS rendering & physics with zero jitter, clamped camera zoom (0.94–1.04), edge-only speed FX, and non-overlapping 96×92 multi-touch hit-zones around Jump/Slide buttons—plus instant Frame-1 auto-start from idle.

Difficulty Modes & Hearts

Smoother opening speeds that scale fairly over distance: Easy (196→400 px/s, 2♥), Medium (216→460 px/s, 1♥), Hard (232→495 px/s, 3♥ + dense hazards), and Practice (immortal shield aura).

4-Skill Loadout & Infinite Skill Lab

Equip up to 4 active skills from 12 total abilities, spawning every 70m–195m by rarity near matching obstacles. Test any skill with unlimited charges inside dedicated Skill Lab arenas.

4 Hazard Traps

Watch out for floating hazard pickups beyond Speed Surge: Speed Surge, Heavy Gravity (1.55× weight), Vision Glitch interference, and temporary Skill Jam.

7-Step Tutorial & Enemies Guide

Input-gated 7-step live tutorial covering every obstacle type, tutorial-only tip cards, and a 9-entry animated Enemies Guide demonstrating jump, slide, dive-stomp, and slash tactics.

Linked Map Builder & PS4 Mode

Build 2.6k / 3.8k / 5.0k custom maps with live jump-arc & gap-passability checks, save/chain multiple maps, swipe the Skin Shop with visual swatches, and play hands-on with full PS4 gamepad navigation.
